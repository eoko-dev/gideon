## About

Gideon is an AI butler for your Discord server. @mention it, reply to it or right-click a message, and it answers in context, remembers each channel and the people in it, catches you up on what you missed, and keeps your reminders and events — while staying inside the channel you're talking in and keeping costs in check.

## Key Features

- **Natural Conversation** - Streamed answers as Discord replies, in any channel, thread or forum post; Gideon knows the date, the server and what you're replying to
- **Tools in Plain Language** - Reminders, events, images, web search and pages, polls, threads, and questions about the channel itself ("what did Sam say about the raid?")
- **Catch-Ups & Message Menus** - `/catchup`, *Summarize from here*, *Ask Gideon*, *Remind me about this*, *Translate*, *Remember this about me*
- **Reminders in Your Timezone** - Repeats, DM delivery, and Snooze / Done buttons
- **Events with RSVP** - Going / Maybe / Can't cards with "starting soon" reminders
- **Memory** - Channel summaries and per-person notes, never shared between channels, with opt-out
- **Per-Channel Personas** - A different name, avatar and voice in every channel
- **Multi-Provider AI** - OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini and 100+ models via OpenRouter
- **Image & Video Generation** - AI Horde, Cloudflare Workers, DALL-E, ComfyUI and OpenRouter
- **AI-Powered Trivia** - Dynamic questions on any topic with scoring and leaderboards
- **Web Admin Dashboard** - Settings, personas, memory, usage and cost controls, backups
